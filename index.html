Formatting Guide

Structure — 5 Building Blocks

  • # Your Name — your name (h1, large centered)
  • > contact line — contact info line (blockquote, centered below name)
  • ## SECTION TITLE — section header (e.g. WORK EXPERIENCE, EDUCATION)
  • ### Company Name || City, State — company row, bold left / location right
  • **Job Title** || Month Year – Present — role row, italic left / date right

Lines: Solid vs Dotted

  • Solid line — appears automatically before a new ### Company that follows a bullet list (between companies)
  • Dotted line — appears automatically before a **Role** that follows a bullet list (between roles at the same company)
  • These are CSS-driven — you don't add them manually

The --- Rule

  • Only use --- before ## SECTION headers (after your contact info, between major sections)
  • Never use --- between roles at the same company — it creates a solid line and can trigger a heading bug
  • To separate sub-roles, just leave a blank line; the dotted line appears automatically

Blank Lines Matter

  • Always leave a blank line between a role/company line and the bullet list below it
  • Always leave a blank line between the last bullet and the next role/company
  • Missing blank lines can cause bullets to merge into headings

Quick Reference — One Company Block

### Company Name || City, State
**First Role** || Jan 2022 – Present

- Accomplishment one.
- Accomplishment two.

**Earlier Role** || Jun 2020 – Dec 2021

- Accomplishment one.
- Accomplishment two.

The dotted line before "Earlier Role" appears automatically — no --- needed.
